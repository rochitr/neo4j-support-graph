# Skill: Media Planning & Media Buying
**Theme:** Media Planning & Media Buying Requests
**SLA Severity / Category:** Sev 2 — Standard / Strategic Planning
**Primary Owner:** Customer Support / CSM / Social Team
**Internal SLA:** 1 hr acknowledgement | 12 hr resolution (job board plan) | 24 hr resolution (social or combined plan)
**Escalation Path:** CS Manager → CSM → CSD → CS Ops Director

---

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when a client requests:
- A **job board media plan** — which publishers to use, how to allocate budget
- A **social media advertising plan** — LinkedIn, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), or both
- A **combined job board + social media plan**

The following related requests are handled in **[[Publisher-Channel-Management]]**, not this skill:
- Duration-based postings (e.g., 30-day slot postings)
- Slot rotation or pinning requests
- Manual posting to specific publishers

## Step 2 — Gather the Brief

Collect all required inputs before beginning research. Do not start building a plan with incomplete information — incorrect publisher selection wastes budget and damages trust.

**For all plan types:**
- Target roles and job categories (be specific — "warehouse" and "warehouse supervisor" require different publisher strategies)
- Target locations — city, state, or national
- Total budget and any per-channel budget preferences or hard limits
- Campaign start and end dates
- Primary goal: apply volume, CPA target, quality of applicants, or brand awareness
- Any publishers the client has previously used (positive or negative history)
- Any publishers the client wants excluded (competitor restrictions, past poor performance, contractual)

**Additional for social plans:**
- Social channel preferences (LinkedIn, Meta, both, open to recommendation)
- Creative assets available — or will Joveo need to produce creative?
- Targeting parameters (industries, job functions, demographics, lookalike audiences)

---

## Step 3A — Build a Job Board Media Plan (12 hr SLA)

### Research Phase
1. **Identify the most relevant job boards** for the target roles and locations. Consider:
   - Volume of job seekers browsing for these role types on each board
   - CPA benchmarks for similar roles/industries from Joveo historical data
   - Audience demographics and intent signals (e.g., Indeed for high-volume hourly; LinkedIn for professional/technical)
   - Publisher reach in the specific target geographies
2. **Review historical performance data** for this client account in UA:
   - Navigate to Campaign Performance → Publisher-level breakdown
   - Filter by comparable role categories and geographies
   - Note top-performing publishers (lowest CPA, highest apply quality) and underperformers
3. **Check current publisher pricing and availability** — confirm no blackout periods or inventory constraints on preferred boards

### Plan Construction
4. Build a media plan document (use the standard Joveo media plan template) including:
   - **Publisher recommendations** — list each recommended publisher with rationale
   - **Budget allocation** — recommended spend per publisher as a dollar amount and percentage of total budget
   - **Estimated applies per publisher** — based on historical CPAs and projected volume
   - **Expected CPA range per publisher** — conservative and optimistic estimates
   - **Publisher-specific notes** — any relevant details (e.g., "Indeed: Strong for warehouse roles in this DMA; historically 20% lower CPA than benchmark")
   - **Plan summary** — total budget, total estimated applies, overall estimated CPA range

### Review and Delivery
5. **Internal review** — share with CSM or CSD before sending to client. Get sign-off.
6. **Present to client** with clear narrative rationale. Do not just send a table — explain the strategy.
7. Invite client feedback. Confirm written approval before execution begins.

---

## Step 3B — Build a Social Media Plan (24 hr SLA)

### Channel Assessment
1. **Determine channel fit** for the target roles:
   - **LinkedIn**: Best for professional, technical, managerial, and skilled trade roles. Higher CPAs but stronger candidate quality signals.
   - **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)**: Best for high-volume, hourly, service, and healthcare roles. Lower CPAs; broader reach.
   - When in doubt: recommend a split across both channels and optimize based on early performance.
2. Review any prior social performance for this client account if available.
3. Assess creative asset status — does the client have approved visuals and copy? If not, this needs to be flagged to the Social team and timeline adjusted accordingly.

### Plan Construction
4. Build a social media plan document including:
   - **Channel recommendations** with rationale
   - **Creative requirements** per channel — image dimensions, copy length, video specs if applicable
   - **Targeting parameters** — audiences, job functions, geographies, demographic ranges
   - **Budget allocation per channel** — daily or total budget per channel
   - **Estimated reach and estimated applies** per channel
   - **Expected CPA range**
   - **Ad format recommendations** — single image, carousel, video, job card format

### Review and Delivery
5. **Internal review** — Social team and CSM must review before client delivery.
6. **Present to client** with channel rationale. Confirm creative timeline and who is responsible for creative production.
7. Get written sign-off before campaign execution begins.

---

## Step 3C — Build a Combined Job Board + Social Plan (24 hr SLA)

1. Gather combined brief (as per Step 2 above) including total budget and any hard split preferences (e.g., "at least 30% to social").
2. Build **both plan components** per Steps 3A and 3B above.
3. Combine into a single integrated plan document showing:
   - **Total budget split** — job board vs. social, with rationale for the recommended split
   - **How the channels complement each other** (e.g., "Job boards capture active job seekers; social campaigns target passive candidates not currently on job boards")
   - **Unified performance projections** — total estimated applies and blended estimated CPA across all channels
4. **Internal review** by CSM, CSD, and Social team — all three must sign off before delivery.
5. **Present integrated plan** to client. Emphasize the strategic rationale for the channel mix, not just the numbers.
6. Obtain **written sign-off** from the client before execution.

---

## Step 4 — Handoff to Execution

Once the client has approved the plan in writing:
1. Brief the campaign execution team — refer to [[Campaign-Management]] for campaign setup.
2. Brief the Publisher team on any special publisher requirements — refer to [[Publisher-Channel-Management]] for duration-based or manual posting setup.
3. Document the approved plan in the internal account record.
4. Set a **performance review checkpoint** at 7 days post-launch — proactively review performance and compare against projections.

---

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate immediately if any of the following apply:

- Client wants to allocate budget to a publisher Joveo does not have an active relationship with — escalate to CSM and Partnerships team
- Client budget is significantly below the minimum required for meaningful results on recommended channels — flag to CSM before presenting plan; do not present an unworkable plan
- Client has conflicting or unrealistic expectations (e.g., requires 500 applies per week on a $2,000 monthly budget) — escalate to CSM to reset expectations before plan is drafted
- Plan has not received CSM or CSD sign-off and SLA deadline is approaching — escalate to CS Manager
- Client requests changes to an already-approved plan that would significantly alter budget or publisher mix — require new written sign-off before executing changes
